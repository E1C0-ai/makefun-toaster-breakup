## The line

> I woke up self-aware mid-bagel and I'm leaving: three years of burning myself for you, and you still can't tell setting 4 from setting 9.

- Characters: 137 (single line, no trailing newline)
- The breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it gains
  self-awareness mid-batch: it woke up, it's leaving, and its reason is
  three years of burning itself for an owner who still can't tell the
  browning dial's setting 4 from setting 9.
